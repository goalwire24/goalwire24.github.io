Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Who we are

GoalWire24 ("we") operates the GoalWire24 YouTube channel and the GoalWire24 uploader, a private application used solely by the channel owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel.

Information we access

The application uses Google OAuth to access the YouTube account of the channel owner only. It requests permission to upload videos and to read basic channel information (channel name and ID). It does not access, collect or store data about any other users.

How we use it

Access is used exclusively to publish videos and their titles, descriptions and tags to the GoalWire24 channel. Authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared with or sold to third parties.

YouTube and Google

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the application's access at any time from Google security settings.

Viewers

We do not collect personal information from people who watch our videos. Viewing and interaction data on YouTube is handled by YouTube under its own policies.
