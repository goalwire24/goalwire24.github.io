Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Who we are

GoalWire24 ("we") is an independent creator who operates two YouTube channels, GoalWire24 (football news) and Chisme Chingón MX (Mexican entertainment news), and the GoalWire24 uploader, a private application used solely by the owner to upload videos to those two channels.

Information we access

The application uses Google OAuth to access only the owner's own YouTube channels (GoalWire24 and Chisme Chingón MX), each authorized separately by the owner. It requests permission to upload videos and to read basic channel information (channel name and ID). It does not access, collect or store data about any other users.

How we use it

Access is used exclusively to publish videos and their titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's two channels. Authorization tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repositories, where the scheduled publishing runs, and on the owner's computer. They are never shared with or sold to third parties.

YouTube and Google

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the application's access at any time from Google security settings.

Data retention and deletion

The only data the application keeps is (1) the OAuth authorization token and (2) the IDs of the videos it uploaded to the owner's channels, kept in an upload log. Both are stored only in the owner's private GitHub repositories (the token as an encrypted secret) and on the owner's computer. No other YouTube or Google user data is stored.

You can delete this data at any time: revoking the application's access at Google security settings immediately invalidates the token; the application stops working for that channel and the owner deletes the stored token. Uploaded videos can be deleted from YouTube Studio. Requests to delete the upload log or any other data can be sent to proyectobrk02@gmail.com and will be completed within 7 days.

Viewers

We do not collect personal information from people who watch our videos. Viewing and interaction data on YouTube is handled by YouTube under its own policies.
